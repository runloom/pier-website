Pier 0.1.62 (2026-11-18) is released. This release includes the following updates.

## Fixes

- **When dragging a tab to the top, bottom, left, or right of the terminal panel, the drop preview is restored to a true semi-transparent highlight.** The semi-transparent fill and 1px border are now drawn by the terminal compositing layer, so terminal content continues to show through; this applies to both same-window drags and cross-window moves.
- **Image previews in Git review are automatically re-signed after expiration.** After the 10-minute image ticket expires, reopening or swiping through the preview no longer leaves it permanently stuck on a loading failure.
- **Toast status icons are no longer pushed off-center by Sonner's negative margins.** The success checkmark, info, warning, error, and loading icons are back in the center of their icon slots.
