Pier 0.1.32 (2026-08-26) reworks the session input. Attachments are no longer blank tiles, and unsent content survives closing the input.

## Attachments show what they are

- Attachments sit in a row of small squares above the input: images show a thumbnail, text shows the first lines, other files show a type icon, with the name in a hover tooltip.
- Numbering like `#1` and `#2` is gone.
- Extra attachments scroll sideways instead of crowding the input.

## Mentions and commands look consistent

Paths, commands, skills, attachments, and review mentions in the input now share one rounded capsule style.

## Close and reopen — the draft stays

- Reopening the input puts the caret at the end of the draft, like a typical chat box. Clicking in the body still places the caret where you clicked.
- Close and reopen, and paths, commands, skills, attachments, and review mentions come back — they don't collapse into plain text. A successful send still clears the input.

## Esc in two steps

With a command, skill, or path list open, Esc dismisses only the list. Press it again to close the input.

Update, then open any session. Full changelog: [CHANGELOG](https://github.com/runloom/pier/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
