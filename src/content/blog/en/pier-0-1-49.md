Pier 0.1.49 (2026-10-21) has been released. This release includes the following updates.

## Fixes

- **Large-change reviews no longer fall back to diff highlighting on the renderer main thread.** If the worker times out or is unavailable, review surfaces for patches over 16 files or 256KB retain the worker (waiting up to 30 seconds); if it still fails, they show a retryable error instead of redoing the work inline and freezing the UI. Unresponsiveness diagnostics now include renderer CPU/memory, the `sample` stack from before the crash, and diff worker failure breadcrumbs.
